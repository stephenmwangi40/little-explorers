Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026

Little Explorers is an app for children aged 3 to 6 and their families. We designed it to collect as little as possible. In short: the app does not send any personal information to us or to anyone else.

Information we collect

None from the app. The app does not collect, store on our servers, share or sell any personal information from children or adults. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking, no chat and no location.

Information stored on your device

To work, the app keeps the following only on your device:

  • A nickname such as "Explorer 1" (no real name is needed), an age band (3, 4, 5 or 6) and an avatar colour and hat.
  • Learning progress: levels finished, stars, stickers and answers used to adapt difficulty.
  • Minutes played per day, and your settings.

We never ask for a child's real name, birthday, photo, voice, email address or location.

Purchases

Explorer Plus is sold through the Apple App Store or Google Play. Apple or Google handles payment. We never see your card details. The store only tells the app whether a subscription is active.

Reminders (notifications)

Optional reminders for grown-ups are off by default. If you turn them on, your device schedules them locally. Nothing is sent to us or to any server, and no notification is ever addressed to your child. You can turn them off in Grown-ups → Settings or in your device settings.

Spoken narration

Tiko's voice uses your device's built-in text-to-speech. The app sends only the words to read to that engine on your device.

Third parties

The app contains no third-party advertising, analytics or tracking software, and does not use the advertising identifier.

How long information is kept

InformationKept
Profiles, progress, stickers, settingsOn your device until you delete a profile, choose "Delete all data", or uninstall the app
Minutes playedThe last 60 days; older days are deleted automatically
Answer history for each skillThe last 30 answers; older answers are deleted automatically

Your choices and rights

  • See your data: Grown-ups → Settings → Export data copies everything stored, as text.
  • Delete your data: Grown-ups → Settings → Delete all data (or remove one child). Uninstalling the app also deletes everything.
  • Because we hold no data on our servers, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

Children's privacy laws

We follow the principles of the US Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the EU and UK GDPR and the UK Age Appropriate Design Code, and the Australian Privacy Act: data minimisation, high-privacy defaults and no nudges to share data. The app follows the Apple Kids Category guidelines and the Google Play Families Policy.

Support requests (website only)

If a grown-up contacts us through the support page, we store the topic, the messages you write, and optionally the device type and app version. We don't ask for a name or email address. You get a request number and a private code; the code is stored only in scrambled (hashed) form. To prevent spam, a scrambled version of your internet address is kept for 24 hours. Closed requests are deleted automatically 12 months after the last message, or sooner if you ask us in the request.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above, and describe the change in the app's release notes. We will never start collecting children's data without verifiable parental consent.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Contact us with the form on the support page.
