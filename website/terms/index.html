The app

Little Explorers is an educational app for children aged 3 to 6, used with a parent or carer. The free version includes the daily adventure and the first level of each world. Explorer Plus unlocks every level.

Subscriptions (Explorer Plus)

  • Explorer Plus is an auto-renewing subscription, available as Yearly (12 months) or Monthly (1 month), each with a 7-day free trial. Prices are shown in the app before you buy and may differ by country.
  • Payment is charged to your Apple ID or Google Play account when the free trial ends.
  • The subscription renews automatically unless it is cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Your account is charged for renewal within 24 hours before the end of the current period.
  • Manage or cancel any time in your account settings: on iPhone or iPad, Settings → your name → Subscriptions; on Android, Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.
  • Any unused part of a free trial ends when you buy a subscription. Refunds are handled by Apple or Google under their policies.
  • Use "Restore purchases" in the app to restore Explorer Plus on a new device.
