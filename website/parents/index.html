For parents and carers

Little Explorers is built around what research says helps young children learn from apps: active thinking, no distractions, meaningful stories, and playing together.

How a day works

  • Today's Adventure (10, 15, 20 or 30 minutes, you choose): letters → numbers → shapes → story → science → movement → Rest.
  • The session ends on a calm Rest screen. More time needs a grown-up, through the parental gate.
  • Default daily limit: 30 minutes. Optional bedtime lock from 7 pm to 7 am. These work alongside iOS Screen Time and Google Family Link.

What we never do

  • No ads, no in-game currencies, no loot boxes or random prizes.
  • No streaks, countdowns or characters who are sad when your child leaves.
  • No push notifications to children and no auto-play of the next activity.
  • Children never see prices or "ask your parents" prompts. Plans live behind the parental gate.

How it teaches

  • Phonics in a systematic order: s a t p i n, then m d g o c k, and on to vowel teams like ai, ee and oa.
  • Mastery: a skill is secure after 8 of the last 10 answers are right without hints, across at least 2 days.
  • Hints, never failure: hear it again, then see it shown, then do it together. There is no fail screen.
  • Feedback right away: a clear banner and a word from Tiko after every answer.

The parental gate

Grown-up areas (settings, reports, purchases, extra time, adding a child) open only after tapping numbers that are written as words, for example "seven · two · nine". The numbers change every time.

Accessibility

  • Every instruction is spoken aloud and shown as a caption.
  • Large touch targets (64 points or more), left-handed mode, slow narration, reduce motion, and support for larger system text.
  • Colour is never the only cue: shapes, words and icons are used too.
